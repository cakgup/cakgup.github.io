IMG Adventure

IMG Adventure is an outdoor adventure organization that promotes holistic well-being. I led the development of its online portal, streamlined event registration, and implemented security best practices to protect user data.

Visit Website

Project Overview

Indonesia Maju Gemilang (IMG) Adventure embraces the philosophy of "Fit Body, Fit Mind, Fit Soul, and Fit Organization." As its membership grew and its events expanded, the organization needed a centralized digital platform to manage expedition registrations, participant health records, and community engagement. Protecting participants' sensitive personal and medical data was a core requirement.

I designed and developed the organization's web portal with security as a priority. I set up a secure cloud environment, implemented encryption for user records, and deployed web application firewalls (WAFs) to protect against common online threats. I also established IT governance procedures to support ongoing compliance and the secure management of digital assets.
