Who I Am About Me I'm Cak Gup (Mukhammad Gufron Ikhsan), a cybersecurity and IT systems professional with a master's degree in information security engineering from Universitas Indonesia. I currently work as an IT specialist in the Treasury's data management and security unit.

Professional focus: My work spans cybersecurity governance, penetration testing, IT infrastructure security, cloud architecture, and IT service management (ITSM). I hold certifications from Google, EC-Council, Cisco, and BSSN, and contribute to IT security frameworks, electronic government (SPBE) implementation, and application development initiatives.

Beyond technology, I'm passionate about sustainable development, community service, mountaineering, Wing Chun, and wellness therapy. I welcome opportunities to collaborate on security projects and community initiatives. Feel free to contact me.
