Project Overview

Baghasasi Foundation is a nonprofit organization based in Bekasi City. Its name combines BAKTI (Service), GUNA (Benefit), HARAPAN BANGSA (Hope for the Nation), and SINERGI (Synergy). Its mission centers on community empowerment, educational support, and disaster relief. To reach more people and work more efficiently, the foundation needed an updated digital presence and simpler administrative workflows.

As the foundation's IT administrator and secretary, I led its digital transformation initiative. I developed and secured its official WordPress website, set up a centralized communication platform using Google Workspace, and established data management practices for volunteer and donor records. I also led the graphic design work, creating visual campaigns that helped increase community participation and donor contributions.
